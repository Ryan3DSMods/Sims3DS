Good to know

    
    
      
  • What you need: a New 3DS, New 3DS XL or New 2DS XL with custom firmware (Luma3DS and FBI; see 3ds.hacks.guide) and your own The Sims Complete Collection.
    • 
      
  • What ends up on the card: 3ds/sims/ (the game files from your copy and sims.3dsx) and cia/sims.cia. The files contain your game, so keep them for yourself and don't share them.
    • 
      
  • Then: on the 3DS open FBI, then SD, cia, sims.cia, Install CIA. Start sims3ds from the HOME Menu. The first start takes 1–2 minutes.
    • 
      
  • Saves are safe: running the builder again onto the same card keeps your Saves folder.
    • 
      
  • Prefer a script? The extractor in the release zip does the same on your computer.
